What is EOQ?

The Economic Order Quantity is the order size that balances two competing costs:

  1. Ordering cost: a fixed cost every time you place an order.
  2. Holding cost: the cost of keeping each unit in the warehouse.

Formula: EOQ = √(2 × D × S ÷ H)

The inputs

Symbol Meaning Example
D Annual demand (units per year) 12 000
S Ordering cost per order R50
H Holding cost per unit per year R2

With these numbers, EOQ ≈ 774.6 units, about 15.49 orders per year.
